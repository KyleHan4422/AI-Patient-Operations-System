## How We Handle Insurance

For plans we are contracted with, we file claims on your behalf and you pay
only your share at the visit. For other plans, we can still treat you: you pay
at the time of the visit and we give you an itemised receipt with the treatment
codes your insurer needs, which most companies accept for reimbursement.

Bring your insurance card to every visit. Coverage changes more often than
patients expect, usually in January.

## In-Network and Out-of-Network

Being in network means the practice has agreed with an insurer on a set fee for
each treatment. You pay the share your plan defines, and the insurer pays the
rest directly.

Out of network means no such agreement exists. Your plan may still pay part of
the cost, but it reimburses at its own rate, and the difference between that
rate and our fee is yours to pay. This difference, not the treatment, is what
usually surprises people.

## Estimates Are Not Bills

Before any treatment that is more than routine, we prepare a written estimate:
what is planned, what we expect your insurer to cover, and what we expect you
to pay.

An estimate is a good-faith prediction, not a guarantee. Insurers make the
final decision after the claim is filed, and they occasionally decide
differently than the plan documents suggest. If that happens, we will tell you
what changed and why before anything is billed to you.

## Ways to Pay

We take debit and credit cards, cash, and health savings or flexible spending
accounts. We do not take personal cheques. Payment is due at the visit unless
an arrangement has been made in advance.

## Payment Plans

For larger treatment plans we offer third-party financing, applied for at the
front desk, usually with a decision in a few minutes. Interest-free periods are
common for shorter terms. We do not run our own credit checks and do not report
to credit agencies; the financing company does.

If financing is not a good fit, ask about staging treatment instead. Many plans
can be split across several months so that the most urgent work is done first.

## Questions About Your Own Plan

Ask us. We verify benefits before major treatment, and the front desk can check
what your plan covers, whether we are in network with it, and what your
remaining annual maximum is. Please do not rely on a general description like
this page for a decision about money.
