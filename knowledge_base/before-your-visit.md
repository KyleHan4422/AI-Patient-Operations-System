How to prepare for an appointment, and what we need to know in advance.

## Eating and Drinking

Eat normally before a routine appointment or a filling. A light meal beforehand
is actually helpful, because you may not want to eat for a few hours after
treatment.

Fasting is only required for treatment under sedation, and we will tell you
exactly how long beforehand when that is planned.

## Your Medicines

Tell us about every medicine you take, including ones that seem unrelated to
teeth, and any that changed since your last visit. Blood thinners, medicines
for osteoporosis, immune-suppressing treatment and diabetes medication all
change how we plan treatment.

Do not stop any prescribed medicine before a dental appointment on your own.
If a change is needed, it is a decision for the doctor who prescribed it, and
we will contact them.

## Antibiotics Before Treatment

A small number of patients are advised to take an antibiotic an hour before
certain dental treatment, usually because of a specific heart condition or a
joint replacement. If you have been told this applies to you, say so when you
book, not on the day: the prescription has to be in hand before the
appointment.

## Sedation

For patients who need it, we offer inhaled sedation, which wears off within
minutes, and oral sedation, which does not. If you are having oral sedation you
will need someone to bring you home, and you should not drive, work or look
after small children for the rest of the day.

## If You Are Anxious

Tell us. It is the single most useful thing an anxious patient can do, and
nobody here finds it unusual. We can book a longer appointment so nothing is
rushed, agree on a hand signal to stop, explain each step before it happens, or
start with a visit where no treatment is done at all.

## If You Are Unwell or Pregnant

Reschedule if you have a fever, an active infection or a stomach illness. There
is no charge for cancelling because you are ill, whenever you call.

Routine dental care is safe during pregnancy and gum problems are more common
then, so do not put off a cleaning. Tell us which trimester you are in and
whether your pregnancy has any complications, so we can choose timing and
positioning that are comfortable.
