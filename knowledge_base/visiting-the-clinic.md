Getting here, parking, and what the practice is like when you arrive.

## Finding Us

Riverside Family Dental is on the ground floor of a mixed residential building
on Riverside Drive, on the Upper West Side of Manhattan. The entrance is the
glass door beside the pharmacy, not the residential lobby, which is the thing
first-time visitors most often get wrong.

The practice name is on the door and on the awning.

## Parking

There is metered street parking on the avenue, which is easiest in the middle
of the day and difficult at school drop-off and pick-up times.

A paid parking garage sits one block north. We validate garage parking for
appointments longer than an hour; bring the ticket to the front desk before you
leave. We cannot validate meters or tickets from other garages.

If you are being driven, there is a loading area directly outside where a car
can wait briefly.

## Public Transport

The nearest subway stop is a five-minute walk, and two crosstown bus routes
stop within a block. Coming from the east side, the crosstown bus is usually
faster than the subway at any time of day.

## Accessibility

The entrance is step-free and the door is wide enough for a wheelchair. The
treatment rooms and the toilet are on one level, with no internal steps.

Tell us when you book if you use a wheelchair or a walker, or if transferring
to a dental chair is difficult, so we can set aside a room with more space and
allow extra time.

## Languages

Between the front desk and the clinical team we speak English, Spanish and
Mandarin. For other languages, we can arrange a phone interpreter if you tell
us when booking. Family members are welcome to interpret, though for consent
conversations we prefer a professional interpreter.

## The Waiting Area

There is free Wi-Fi; the password is printed at the front desk. Water and tea
are available. The waiting area is small, so we ask that only one person comes
with you, unless you are bringing children.
