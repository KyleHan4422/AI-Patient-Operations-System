What to do after treatment, and what is worth a phone call. When in doubt,
call us: we would much rather answer a question than treat a complication.

## After a Filling

You can eat as soon as the numbness is gone. Eating while numb is the main way
people bite their cheek or tongue, so wait the two to three hours if you can,
and give children something soft once they can feel their lip again.

Mild sensitivity to cold for a few days is normal. Sensitivity that gets worse
over a week, or pain when biting down, means the filling may need adjusting:
call us, because it is a five-minute fix.

## After an Extraction

The first twenty-four hours matter most. Bite gently on the gauze for about
thirty minutes. Then, for the rest of the day: no rinsing, no spitting, no
smoking, and no drinking through a straw. All four can dislodge the clot that
is forming in the socket.

Keep food soft and lukewarm. Use an ice pack on the cheek in twenty-minute
intervals for the first day to limit swelling. Sleep with your head slightly
raised.

From the second day, rinse gently with warm salt water a few times a day,
especially after eating. Brush the other teeth normally and avoid the socket
itself for a few days.

Some oozing on the first day is expected. Bleeding that soaks through fresh
gauze after an hour is not.

### Dry Socket

A severe, throbbing ache that starts two to four days after an extraction, often
spreading to the ear, can mean the clot has been lost. This is uncomfortable but
easily treated: we clean and dress the socket and the pain usually settles
quickly. Do not wait it out.

## After a Crown

A temporary crown is held with soft cement so it can be removed later. Avoid
sticky sweets and chewing gum on that side, and floss by pulling the floss out
sideways rather than up, so you do not lift the temporary off.

If a temporary crown comes off, keep it and call us. It is not an emergency,
but the tooth should be re-covered within a day or two so it does not move.

After the permanent crown is fitted, some tenderness in the gum is normal for a
few days. If the bite feels high, or you find yourself chewing on the other
side without thinking about it, come in for an adjustment.

## After a Deep Cleaning

Gums may be tender and teeth sensitive to cold for several days. Rinse with
warm salt water, and use a soft brush gently along the gum line rather than
avoiding the area, which slows healing.

Some gum shrinkage after treatment is normal and a sign of inflammation
resolving, though it can make teeth look longer or leave spaces that catch
food.

## When to Call Us

Call if you have bleeding that does not stop with steady pressure, swelling
that keeps growing after two days, a fever, pain that is getting worse instead
of better after three days, or a reaction to a prescribed medicine.
