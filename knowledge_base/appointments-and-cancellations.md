How appointments are booked, changed and cancelled, and what happens when one
is missed.

## Booking an Appointment

Appointments can be booked by phone, in person at the front desk, or through
the assistant on our website. We will ask what the visit is for, because
different treatments need different amounts of chair time and, sometimes, a
different provider.

Routine check-ups are usually booked a few weeks out. If you are in pain, say
so when you book: we keep room for urgent visits and will not ask you to wait
for the next routine opening.

## Changing or Cancelling

We ask for at least 24 hours notice to change or cancel an appointment. That is
enough time for us to offer the slot to someone who is waiting, which is the
whole reason the policy exists.

Cancellations can be made by phone, or by replying to the reminder message we
send the day before. A cancellation left as a voicemail overnight still counts
as notice, as long as it arrives 24 hours before the appointment.

## Late Arrivals

If you arrive more than fifteen minutes late, we may not be able to complete
the planned treatment, because the appointment after yours belongs to someone
else. We will do what fits in the remaining time and book a second visit for
the rest, at no extra charge for splitting it.

If you are running late, call. Sometimes the schedule absorbs it easily, and we
would rather tell you that than have you turn around.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment is one that is not cancelled and not attended. The first
one is noted and nothing more. After repeated missed appointments we may ask
for a deposit to hold future bookings, which is credited against the treatment.

Missed appointments for children are never charged.

## Same-Day Openings and the Waitlist

Ask to be added to the waitlist when you book. If an earlier slot opens up, we
call people on the waitlist in order. This works well for routine visits and is
the fastest way to be seen sooner without a cancellation of your own.
